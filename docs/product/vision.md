# Product vision

Polyglot helps learners build English grammar knowledge through structured lessons, practice, and review. The long-term differentiator is an adaptive curriculum informed by a grammar concept graph and recurring learner errors.

The current product is an early foundation: an A1/A2 grammar curriculum, account sessions, a fixed placement assessment, answer feedback, and a rule-based learning path. Assessment results are estimates from a small question bank, not definitive claims about a learner's level or misconceptions.

Future work should improve evidence quality and explainability before adding statistical models or AI-generated decisions. AI-assisted explanations and conversation practice remain future work.
